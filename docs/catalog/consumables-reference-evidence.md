## Source limitations

1. Oil volume uses the supplier's **literal native size label** (`2 Gallons`, `1 Gallon`, `32oz`). The source does not consistently identify the gallon/ounce convention, the physical package count or the container dimensions. No `L`/`mL` conversion and no bottle-count assumption are inserted. The red-dyed two-gallon item is a genuine **separate listing**, not a claim that the formulation has different performance.
2. Battery product titles use the exact literal chemistry marker LIFE (not a verified cell-composition specification), **9.9 V**, **3800 mAh** or **2100 mAh**. A nominal 9.9 V rating may suggest a 3S configuration, but this is an inference, **not a manufacturer-confirmed cell count**. The source titles do not establish exact LiFePO₄ cell construction, series count, discharge C-rating, connector, charger instructions, specific engine application or safety certification. Third-party retailers' lookalikes are *not* treated as the official source SKU. Both records keep `cellCount: null`, `connector: null`, `compatibleApplications: []`.
3. [850mAh-9.9V LIFE Battery, ID 27](https://www.kingtechturbines.com/product/850mah-9-9v-life-battery/27) was excluded by the 2026-10-23 inventory audit because the original page rendered an application-level 404 (HTTP 200 transport alone is not proof of a live product). The link is preserved only as **exclusion evidence**, not a seventh staged SKU.
4. The source describes turbine lubricant usage for the oil family, but this does not constitute BODICH product compatibility or a lubricant mixing/servicing instruction. Seller-only promotion, price, availability and U.S. shipping restrictions are not BODICH assurances. Final original manufacturer documentation is still needed before any published technical or safety claim.
