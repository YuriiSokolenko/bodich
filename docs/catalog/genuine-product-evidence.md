Parent epic: #24. Prerequisites: Stage 13 #49 (review ledger), #51 / PR #52 (versioned, sealed review epochs).

**Status: blocked pilot; zero approved products.** This is a public, safe-metadata *contract*, not a legal determination or an inventory claim. The 132 frozen KingTech supplier references remain independent unpublished research. They are not 132 BODICH products. No supplier photo, seller listing, generic BODICH render or draft marketing copy authorizes product/branding/sales claims.

## Intake contract

The committed file `docs/catalog/genuine-product-evidence.json` pins `ledgerEpoch` and all **four current manifest revisions** to Stage 13. A changed inventory, overrides, art manifest or copy matrix must use the **reviewed epoch migration procedure** in `review-ledger-runbook.md`; seal the old history, reconcile added/retired and changed IDs, reset effective approvals, and update the frozen release-reconciliation fixtures in `scripts/reconcile-catalog-release.mjs` when necessary. Do **not** silently edit an existing event, alter an archive or inherit approval from an old epoch. The intake epoch must then be deliberately updated and all evidence rechecked.

An optional intake record links to **one existing source ID** while storing an independent *proposed*, not established, `proposedBodichIdentity: {manufacturer, model, variant}`. It has `evidence: []` and `unresolved: []` until real evidence is supplied. Do not create empty/default reviewer identities, human decision timestamps or event IDs; only genuine human reviewers may add decisions to the **existing** Stage 13 ledger via its append-only CLI, in a separately reviewed PR.

Each evidence entry requires:
- Unique `id`, `gate` and `kind`; exact `scope: {manufacturer, model, variant}` **of the proposed BODICH product**.
- `ref` to a controlled original (**opaque `restricted:` identifier required**; public supplier URLs cannot be intake evidence), `sha256` of the original version/asset, `version`, safe `provenance`, `providerRole`, an opaque `providerRef` identifying who supplied it off-repo, and `providedOn`. All evidence, provider, manufacturer-authorization and image-permission references must be opaque `restricted:` pointers into separately controlled private storage. Even a valid pointer does not prove what a referenced document says.
- `verifiedOn` (calendar date) **only after reinspection**, or `null` until checked, and optional `expiresOn`. Who inspected an original and with what authority is established separately in the attributed Stage 13 human ledger; `verifiedOn` itself is not a reviewer identity. The presence of a date or hash alone is **not authentication or a legal approval**.
- `facts` particular to its gate. Manufacturer authorization must identify origin and an independent authority reference; photo evidence must be a photograph of the **exact** product with asset digest, rights owner and separate written permission reference. AI concepts, illustrations and vendor photographs cannot qualify as a product photograph. Technical evidence must provide individual numeric `name`, `value` and `unit` with its own evidence/provenance/date. Editorial evidence needs separately verified EN copy, native-RO review, legal review and commercial review, each bound to the **same** SHA-256 digest of the current unpublished source-draft EN/RO text; changed source copy invalidates all four reviews.
- **Deterministic editorial fingerprint:** SHA-256 (lowercase hexadecimal) of UTF-8 JSON serialized in this exact field order: `{en:{name,summary,description},ro:{name,summary,description}}`, taking all six string values directly from the current `reference-overrides.json` source ID. This binding is to the **current draft text**, not evidence of a legally approved BODICH statement. This digest is calculated over the working-tree `reference-overrides.json` copy text, **not** over `copy-review-matrix.json`. The copy-matrix revision is separately pinned by the ledger epoch; changing either input requires re-review.
- **Exact-image digest binding:** for an authentic photograph or exact-model technical illustration, top-level `sha256` **must equal** `facts.assetSha256`, which must match the current-epoch ledger's imagery asset digest. Concept/illustration and supplier images never satisfy the required exact-model product-photo gate.
- Supplier-only detection from free text is **best effort**, not a reliable proof of manufacturer identity: explicit supplier indicators are rejected, and external URLs are disallowed as evidence refs, but a human must inspect original documents, authorship, chain of rights, variant and legal authorization in private controlled storage before signing off. Neither an opaque ID nor a cryptographic hash authenticates a source by itself.
- **Defensive validation:** free-text evidence metadata rejects common credential assignments and `Bearer`-style authorization tokens. Specification contradiction checks normalize measurement names (trim, Unicode NFKC, lower case, collapsed whitespace) without guessing unit conversions; differently expressed units remain separate and are conservatively flagged when their values conflict.
- Each unresolved contradiction, doubtful claim, incorrect variant or rights dispute is recorded as `{gate, code, note, evidenceIds}` and blocks the relevant gate. Missing, expired, unverified and mismatched-scope evidence also blocks. All fields are schema validated; unknown fields fail.

**Only safe metadata belongs on public GitHub.** Never upload original contracts, signatures, personal IDs, customer data, photographs with unlicensed rights, source private files, credentials or tokenized links. Keep documents in controlled private storage and use non-identifying `restricted:` pointers. Never claim BODICH ownership from a matching KingTech reference. All evidence is **subject to independent human inspection**; automated validators can check consistency, not authenticity or permission.

## Four separate gates; no second approval engine

`scripts/catalog-genuine-evidence.mjs` reports evidence completeness and gap reason codes for the Stage 13 gates: `identity`, `imagery`, `specifications`, `editorial`. It does **not** approve or change gates. Stage 13's attributed append-only reviewer decisions remain the sole human decision workflow. Promotion is blocked unless the intake is current, the scope and rights are complete, all relevant contradictions resolved, four current-epoch ledger gates are human-approved, and a distinct publication approver signs off. An approval claim unsupported by the independently linked intake fails CI; old-epoch decisions are shown for historical reference only.

This pilot **does not** migrate any draft to `confirmedProducts`, create public product routes, prices, stock, warranties or checkout. A later **separate promotion PR** and real authorized owner/legal/technical/RO rights reviews are mandatory. Browser QA, catalog reconciliation and leak guards must stay green before that work can proceed.

## Current evidence-gap interpretation

For every absent product intake record the offline export provides reason codes such as `missing_bodich_manufacturer_authorization`, `missing_authorized_exact_model_photo`, `missing_independent_model_specifications`, and `missing_en_ro_legal_commercial_review`. Additional blockers distinguish concepts vs photos, changed photo bytes, mismatched EN/RO text digests, contradictory exact-model specifications, wrong variants, expired/unverified evidence, unresolved conflicts and missing current-epoch human decisions. These are **known evidence gaps**, not judgments about whether a future genuine BODICH product exists. This is intentionally a bounded, truthful **blocked pilot**.


## Stage 18 proof extensions (Issue #58)

For independent specifications, optional `facts.metric` (`diameter`/`length` on `dimension`, `turbine`/`propeller` on `speed`) and `facts.scope` (`bare_engine`, `assembly_with_integrated_hardware`, `not_stated` for `mass` only) record the original measurement axis and boundary. Stage 18 rejects any registry claim whose exact qualifier is missing or different in independently verified Stage 14 evidence, even when the numeric quantity and unit match. Existing source-reference evidence remains unchanged; no qualifier may be guessed or backfilled without a real original document.

For Stage 18 BODICH product copy, Stage 14 editorial evidence can also include optional `facts.bodichCopySha256`, SHA-256 of the ordered JSON `{en:{name,summary,description},ro:{name,summary,description}}` of the complete *actual-product registry* text. This digest is **in addition to**, not a replacement for, `facts.copySha256`, which still validates the supplier-source draft against the pinned Stage 13 source. Every verified registry variant requires all four current-epoch Stage 14 editorial kinds to bind that same combined BODICH digest; EN and RO registry review references must point to the matching English/native-Romanian proof entries. Any changed text, expired proof, misbound ref, missing legal/commercial review, or unapproved Stage 13 gate blocks promotion. Neither digests nor opaque pointers constitute human authentication.
